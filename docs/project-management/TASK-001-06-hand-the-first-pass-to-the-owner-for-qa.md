# TASK-001.06: hand the first pass to the owner for QA

Part of TASK-001. This subtask is milestone 6 of the build plan: the final gate run, the
green tree the owner commits and tests, and the hand-off checklist.

## Outcome

- Every line of "Ready for the owner's QA" in the build plan holds on the committed
  tree.
- The owner receives the hand-off checklist: the prerequisites, how to launch the QA
  app, how to move and mark, what only a human can judge, how to check the seals, and
  which two files to send back.
- The owner's marks and feedback open the next iteration.

## Task genesis

Files: the root documents and the task documents. No component source changes in this
subtask.

Read first:

- `TASK-001-deliver-the-dark-factory-level.md`, for the brief, the owner's decisions and
  the rules.
- In `docs/researches/2026-11-16-level-designer-foundation/`: `build-plan.md`
  (subtask 6.1, "Ready for the owner's QA" and "Hand-off checklist").

Rules:

- The owner commits; QA runs that commit. Uncommitted work is never what the owner tests.
- The lead reruns both root gates and reads the repository status before handing the
  tree to the owner.
- The hand-off states what is proved, what is inferred and what is open, in that order.
- The checklist names the file that explains a failure on the owner's machine.
- The lead reports the accepted limits again with the hand-off: no collapse, dead thin
  edges, debris through decks, stale shadows, and hiding spots in wide hollow props.
