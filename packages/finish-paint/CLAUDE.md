# finish-paint rules

- Keep this component free of workspace dependencies, sibling imports, three.js and any
  other package: its source is browser-safe TypeScript with no dependency.
- Write each layer kind once, over the arithmetic interface; use no operation outside its
  fourteen.
- Keep the number back end's every operation rounded to a 32-bit float.
- Count a layer kind's cost in hashes at full detail, whatever its footprint.
